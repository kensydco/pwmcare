Expert Wound Care Services

Evidence-based treatment for acute and chronic wounds with physician oversight and precise clinical intervention.

Comprehensive Diagnostic Evaluation

  • Comprehensive wound evaluation
  • Advanced diagnostic imaging
  • Vascular assessment
  • Infection analysis and culture
  • Wound measurement and photography
  • Treatment plan development

What to Expect

Initial consultation includes complete medical history review, thorough wound examination, wound measurement and photography, diagnostic assessment, and development of a personalized treatment plan. Our comprehensive approach ensures accurate diagnosis and optimal treatment planning.

Insurance

Most diagnostic and assessment services are covered by Medicare, Medicaid, and major insurance plans when medically necessary.

Professional Debridement Procedures

  • Sharp debridement
  • Selective debridement
  • Biofilm management
  • Tissue preparation for healing
  • Removal of necrotic tissue
  • Wound bed preparation

What to Expect

In-office procedure performed by qualified providers with appropriate pain management. Debridement removes dead or infected tissue to promote healing and reduce infection risk. Most procedures are well-tolerated and completed during a standard office visit.

Insurance

Covered for medically necessary procedures under most insurance plans including Medicare and Medicaid.

Advanced Compression Solutions

  • Multi-layer compression systems
  • Graduated compression stockings
  • Pneumatic compression devices
  • Venous insufficiency treatment
  • Edema management
  • Custom compression fitting

What to Expect

Custom fitting and application of compression therapy tailored to your specific condition. Includes comprehensive patient education on proper use, care, and maintenance. Regular follow-up to ensure optimal compression and healing progress.

Insurance

Coverage varies by plan. We provide insurance verification and will work with you to understand your benefits.

Specialized Diabetic Wound Protocols

  • Diabetic foot ulcer treatment
  • Offloading techniques and devices
  • Neuropathy assessment
  • Circulation evaluation
  • Infection prevention and management
  • Patient education and prevention

What to Expect

Comprehensive diabetic foot evaluation including vascular and neurological assessment. Implementation of evidence-based treatment protocols specific to diabetic wounds. Ongoing monitoring and education to prevent complications and promote healing.

Insurance

Generally covered for diabetic patients under Medicare, Medicaid, and most private insurance plans.

Surgical Site Management & Recovery

  • Surgical site monitoring
  • Complication prevention
  • Wound healing optimization
  • Infection surveillance
  • Recovery support and education
  • Coordination with surgical team

What to Expect

Comprehensive post-operative wound monitoring and management in coordination with your surgical team. Regular assessment to detect and prevent complications early. Support throughout your recovery process to ensure optimal healing.

Insurance

Follow-up wound care is typically covered as part of post-operative care under most insurance plans.

Prevention & Advanced Treatment

  • Pressure ulcer staging and assessment
  • Prevention protocols and education
  • Advanced treatment options
  • Support surface evaluation
  • Repositioning and positioning education
  • Caregiver training and support

What to Expect

Comprehensive assessment of pressure injury with staging and treatment planning. Equipment and positioning recommendations to prevent further injury. Caregiver education on prevention strategies and proper care techniques.

Insurance

Covered for medically necessary treatment under Medicare, Medicaid, and most insurance plans.

Specialized Ostomy Care & Skin Protection

  • Ostomy care assessment
  • Skin barrier evaluation
  • Product recommendations and fitting
  • Peristomal skin complications
  • Patient education and support
  • Troubleshooting and problem-solving

What to Expect

Specialized consultation for ostomy-related concerns and skin integrity issues. Assessment of current ostomy system, product trials, and personalized recommendations. Education and support to ensure optimal ostomy management and skin health.

Insurance

Coverage varies by service and plan. We provide insurance verification for ostomy consultations and supplies.
