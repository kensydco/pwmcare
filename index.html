Our Mission

"Precision Wound Management is dedicated to providing expert, evidence-based care for acute and chronic wounds. Our mission is to accelerate healing, reduce complications, and restore quality of life through precise clinical intervention and compassionate service."

Led by Dr. April Carter, MD, JM, MHA, FACP, our physician-led practice brings extensive experience in internal medicine with specialized focus on complex wound management. Working collaboratively with our experienced Nurse Practitioner, Aldridge Westrich, we deliver comprehensive, evidence-based treatment for all types of acute and chronic wounds.
