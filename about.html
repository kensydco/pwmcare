Expert Wound Care Team

Physician-led wound care with a commitment to evidence-based treatment, compassionate service, and optimal healing outcomes for every patient.

Our Mission

"Precision Wound Management is dedicated to providing expert, evidence-based care for acute and chronic wounds. Our mission is to accelerate healing, reduce complications, and restore quality of life through precise clinical intervention and compassionate service."

Clinical Expertise Overview

Our practice combines the clinical oversight of board-certified physician Dr. April Carter with the specialized wound care expertise of Nurse Practitioner Aldridge Westrich. This collaborative, physician-led approach ensures that every patient receives comprehensive assessment, evidence-based treatment planning, and ongoing clinical monitoring.

Quality Commitment

We are committed to the highest standards of wound care through:

  • Evidence-based clinical protocols
  • Advanced diagnostic assessment
  • Specialized treatment modalities
  • Continuous provider education
  • Patient-centered care delivery
  • Collaboration with referring providers

Meet Dr. April Carter

MD, JM, MHA, FACP

Medical Director & CEO

"My calling is healing—guided by compassion, lifelong learning, and patient empowerment."

Board-certified physician providing expert clinical oversight and evidence-based wound care protocols. Extensive experience in internal medicine with specialized focus on complex wound management.

Dr. Carter brings a unique combination of clinical expertise, advanced medical education, and healthcare leadership to Precision Wound Management. Her comprehensive background ensures that patients receive the highest level of diagnostic assessment and treatment planning, with a focus on achieving optimal healing outcomes while maintaining quality of life.

Meet Aldridge Westrich, NP

Nurse Practitioner

Primary Clinical Provider

Experienced Nurse Practitioner specializing in acute and chronic wound care. Works collaboratively under Dr. Carter's oversight to deliver compassionate, evidence-based treatment for complex wound cases.

Aldridge brings extensive clinical experience in wound assessment, treatment implementation, and patient education. His collaborative approach ensures continuity of care while working seamlessly with Dr. Carter to implement comprehensive treatment protocols tailored to each patient's unique needs.

With a focus on patient education and support, Aldridge ensures that patients and their families understand their treatment plan and have the knowledge and resources needed to participate actively in the healing process.

Meet Kenneth Burnett, MBA

MBA

Chief Operating Officer

Brings extensive business leadership and operational expertise to ensure efficient practice management and exceptional patient service delivery. Oversees administrative operations, technology integration, and strategic growth initiatives.

Kenneth's business acumen and commitment to operational excellence ensure that Precision Wound Management maintains the highest standards of patient service while implementing efficient systems and processes. His focus on continuous improvement helps the practice remain at the forefront of wound care delivery.

By managing the administrative and operational aspects of the practice, Kenneth allows the clinical team to focus entirely on patient care while ensuring that every patient experience meets the practice's high standards for accessibility, efficiency, and service quality.

Meet Kimberly Travis

Practice Manager

Kimberly Travis serves as Practice Manager at Precision Wound Management, bringing extensive experience in healthcare administration and patient services. She ensures seamless coordination of all operational aspects of the practice, from patient scheduling and insurance verification to clinical workflow management.

With a strong commitment to patient-centered care, Kimberly oversees the administrative team to ensure that every patient receives prompt, courteous, and efficient service. Her attention to detail and organizational expertise help maintain the high standards of care that define Precision Wound Management.

Kimberly works closely with the clinical team to optimize patient flow, manage referral coordination with healthcare providers, and ensure that all administrative processes support the practice's mission of delivering exceptional wound care outcomes. Her dedication to operational excellence allows the medical staff to focus entirely on patient care while ensuring a smooth and welcoming experience for every patient.
