Medicare

As a Medicare participating provider, we are committed to serving our Medicare beneficiaries with the highest quality wound care services.

Most wound care services are covered under Medicare Part B when medically necessary, including:

  • Wound assessment and evaluation
  • Debridement procedures
  • Compression therapy
  • Diabetic foot ulcer care
  • Post-operative wound management

We will verify your Medicare coverage prior to your appointment.

Medicaid

As a Medicaid participating provider, we are dedicated to ensuring accessible wound care for all members of our community.

Arkansas Medicaid covers medically necessary wound care services, including comprehensive assessment and treatment.

Our staff will work with Medicaid to verify your benefits and ensure prior authorization when required.

Insurance Verification

Our administrative staff will verify your insurance benefits prior to your appointment to help you understand your coverage and any potential out-of-pocket costs.

We Recommend:

  • Calling your insurance company to confirm coverage details for wound care services
  • Asking about any deductibles, co-pays, or co-insurance that may apply
  • Verifying that Precision Wound Management is in-network with your plan
  • Understanding any pre-authorization requirements

If you have questions about coverage, please call us at (870) 559-4252 and our staff will be happy to assist you.

Financial Assistance

We believe quality wound care should be accessible to all patients. If you have concerns about costs or coverage, please speak with our administrative staff about available options.

Our team is committed to working with you to:

  • Understand your insurance benefits
  • Explore payment plan options
  • Answer questions about costs
  • Ensure you receive the care you need

Financial concerns should never prevent you from seeking necessary wound care. Please contact us to discuss your situation.
